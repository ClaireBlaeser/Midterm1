The BTU Lab keeps lots of general tools on hand for all your inventing needs.

General Tools

Available Equipment

We have a wide variety of general tools available for use in the lab. These include hand tools, measuring instruments, and other equipment that can be used for various projects.
Equipment includes (but not limited to):

  • Hammer
  • Screwdriver
  • Wrench
  • Measuring Tape
  • Knives
  • Cutting Mats
  • Glues
  • Tape
  • Sanding tools

Color Coding

General Tools equipment and materials are labeled GREEN.
Power tools, mostly used in the Wood Shop, are more like... GREEEEN!

BTU LAB IS NOT A FREE SUPPLY STORE. But there are many ways to barter, including displaying your work in BTU, tagging us in social media, offering workshops, volunteering to organize supplies, and more. If you need something, we ask that you give something in return.
